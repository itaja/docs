Itaja is a subscription management and recurring billing platform built for African businesses. Instead of chasing customers for payments or building custom integrations with mobile money providers, you connect once and let Itaja handle the rest — from charging subscribers on schedule to retrying failed payments and sending smart reminders. Whether you serve customers in Senegal, Nigeria, Kenya, Côte d'Ivoire, or across the continent, Itaja gives you one unified platform to manage your entire recurring revenue operation.

## Key Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pan-African Reach" icon="globe-africa">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Full Automation" icon="arrows-rotate">
