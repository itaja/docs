Itaja gives your business a single integration point for collecting recurring mobile money payments across sub-Saharan and West Africa. With live coverage spanning 19 countries — from Senegal and Nigeria in the west to Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda in the east — you can launch and grow subscription products across the continent without building or managing separate provider integrations for each market. All countries share the same API, the same webhook events, and the same dashboard.

## Currency Settlement

Itaja settles payments in the local currency of the country where the transaction originates. A subscription billed in Kenya settles in KES; one billed in Senegal settles in XOF. You don't need to manage currency conversion — Itaja handles the full payment cycle in the customer's local currency and passes the settled amount through to you.

If you operate across multiple countries, your Itaja dashboard displays totals grouped by currency so you can track revenue in each market independently. You can also query per-currency breakdowns through the API to feed your own reporting or finance systems.
